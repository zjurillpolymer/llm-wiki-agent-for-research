# Jian Ji

Jian Ji is a corresponding senior author of the PerioGT study on periodicity-aware deep learning for polymers. In the study, Ji helped guide a domain-specific graph-learning framework that connects polymer structural periodicity with property prediction and experimental antimicrobial polymer discovery.
