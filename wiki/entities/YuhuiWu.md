# Yuhui Wu

Yuhui Wu is the first author of the PerioGT study on periodicity-aware deep learning for polymers. Wu and collaborators developed a graph-based self-supervised learning framework that uses polymer periodicity as a structural prior and applied it to polymer property prediction and antimicrobial polymer discovery.
