# Dan Lu

Dan Lu is the first author of the study on data-mechanism-fused design of membranes for organic micropollutant removal. In that work, Lu and collaborators developed the [[DMF-MRL]] framework, combining molecular fingerprints, physical models and interpretable machine learning to analyze [[PolyamideMembranes]].
