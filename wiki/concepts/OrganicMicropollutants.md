# Organic Micropollutants

Organic micropollutants (OMPs) are diverse, often persistent organic chemicals present at trace concentrations in aquatic systems. Their molecular size, charge, functional groups and hydrophobicity vary substantially, which makes universal membrane-screening rules unreliable. The membrane study in this wiki models OMP rejection by combining molecular fingerprints and physical mechanisms rather than treating one functional group as an isolated predictor.
