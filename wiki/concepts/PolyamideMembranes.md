# Polyamide Membranes

Polyamide thin-film composite membranes are a major platform for nanofiltration and reverse osmosis because their small pores and surface-charge properties enable molecular-level separation. The membrane study compares how operating conditions, hydrophobic interaction, size sieving and Donnan exclusion contribute to OMP rejection, and tests charge and pore-architecture modifications for more selective removal.
