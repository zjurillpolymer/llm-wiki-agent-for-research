# DSPM-DE

The Donnan-Steric Pore Model with Dielectric Exclusion (DSPM-DE) describes how membrane pore structure and charge affect solute transport. In the DMF-MRL framework, it contributes size-sieving and Donnan-partition features, allowing the model to distinguish molecular size resistance from charge-based exclusion.
